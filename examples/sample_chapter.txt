When Iris reached the gates of Eldoria, Captain Rowan was already waiting.

"You are late, Little Star," Rowan said, though his tired voice held no anger.

Iris touched the blue Mana Core beneath her cloak. "The Ash Council sent riders
to every road. If they learn what this can awaken, the village will not survive
the night."

Rowan looked toward the ancient forest. "Then we take the northern path. Stay
close, Iris."

"As you command, Captain."
